Undergraduate Thesis Research | Ongoing

Evaluation of the Reliability and Validity of a Sensor-Based Device for Gait Analysis

This ongoing undergraduate thesis project evaluates the reliability and validity of a seven-sensor inertial measurement unit (IMU)-based gait-analysis system across stroke, osteoarthritis, and healthy populations. The study includes 59 participants and combines IMU-based gait analysis with clinical gait assessments, including the 10-Meter Walk Test and Dynamic Gait Index.
